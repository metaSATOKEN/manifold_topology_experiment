## Historical cell and notebook hashes

The stage release manifests retain hashes of the original compressed Colab cells and notebooks. Those duplicate entry-point files are omitted from this publication tree; their full unpacked contents are retained and mapped in [SOURCE_EQUIVALENCE.json](../references/SOURCE_EQUIVALENCE.json). Historical protocols and source checks may mention the old filenames. Use the [source-code guide](CODE_GUIDE.md) and [current package manifest](../references/MANIFEST.json) for the published layout. The scientific sources and historical manifests have not been rewritten.
